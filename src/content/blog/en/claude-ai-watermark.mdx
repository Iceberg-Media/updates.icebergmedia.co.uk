Claude now marks all AI-generated content with invisible watermarks and signed metadata. If you use Claude for content, your audience can detect it.

## What Changed

Anthropic signed the EU AI Act's Article 50(2) Code of Practice. Starting August 2, 2026, all new Claude models mark content at launch:

- **Invisible watermarks** embedded in text — travel with copy/paste, persist through some editing
- **C2PA metadata** attached to files (.svg, .png, .jpg) — follows open standard for content provenance
- **Works everywhere** — Claude API, Claude.ai, Claude Code, AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Foundry

## Why This Matters for SEO

Google's helpful content system penalises low-quality AI content. Watermarked content makes it easier to identify:

- **Search engines** can potentially detect watermarked content
- **Readers** can verify if content was AI-generated
- **Clients** may ask if your content is AI-assisted

## What You Can Do Now

**If you use Claude for content:**
- Review output before publishing — add your own voice and expertise
- Don't rely on AI for E-E-A-T signals (experience, expertise, authoritativeness, trustworthiness)
- Mix AI drafts with original research, data, and case studies

**If you create content for clients:**
- Disclose AI assistance if asked
- Ensure human oversight on all published content
- Focus on unique insights AI can't generate

## What's Limitied

Watermarks aren't foolproof:

- Heavy editing can remove or degrade the watermark
- Short passages may not carry a detectable signal
- File metadata can be stripped through format conversion
- Pre-August 2026 Claude models don't mark content yet
