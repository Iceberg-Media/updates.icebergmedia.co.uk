Real businesses have listings on Yell, Thomson Local, Checkatrade, TrustATrader, Yelp, and more. Google checks these. Get listed on every relevant UK directory.

## What Are Citations?

Citations are mentions of your business name, address, and phone number on other websites. They don't need to include a link — just your NAP information.

## Why Citations Matter

Google uses citations to verify your business exists and is located where you say it is. More citations = more trust = higher rankings.

## UK Directories to Target

### General Directories
- Yell.com
- Thomson Local
- Yelp
- Facebook Business
- LinkedIn Company Page

### Trade-Specific
- Checkatrade
- TrustATrader
- MyBuilder
- Bark
- Rated People

### Local Directories
- Local council business directories
- Chamber of Commerce
- Local business associations

## How to Build Citations

1. **Claim existing listings.** Search for your business and claim any unclaimed listings.
2. **Create new listings.** Submit your business to directories you're not on.
3. **Keep NAP consistent.** Use the exact same information everywhere.
4. **Add photos and descriptions.** Complete profiles rank better.
