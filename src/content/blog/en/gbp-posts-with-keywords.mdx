Every Google Business Profile post should include your keyword and your location. Every single time. Google reads these posts for relevance signals.

## Why GBP Posts Matter

Google uses GBP posts to understand what your business does and where it operates. Posts with keywords and locations rank better.

## How to Write Effective GBP Posts

1. **Include your primary keyword.** If you're a plumber, use "plumber" in the post.

2. **Include your location.** "Emergency plumber in Stockport" — not "Check out our latest work."

3. **Use natural language.** Don't keyword stuff. Write for humans, optimise for Google.

4. **Add images.** Posts with images get more engagement.

5. **Include a CTA.** Tell people what to do next.

## Post Types That Work

- **Offers** — Seasonal promotions, discounts
- **Updates** — News about your business
- **Events** — Local events you're involved in
- **Products** — Highlight specific services

## UK-Specific Tips

- Reference local events and seasons
- Use British English
- Include local postcodes
- Mention local landmarks
