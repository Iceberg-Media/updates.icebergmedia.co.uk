Review count is the single biggest ranking factor for the Google Map Pack. If you have more reviews than every competitor in your area, you will rank higher. It's that simple.

## Why Reviews Matter More Than Anything Else

Google's local algorithm weights review count heavily. Reviews from the last 90 days carry even more weight — Google sees recent reviews as a signal that your business is active and relevant.

## How Many Reviews Do You Need?

The answer is: more than every competitor in your area. Search for your primary service in your city and count the reviews on the top 3 results. That's your target.

## Practical Steps to Get More Reviews

1. **Ask every customer.** The number one reason businesses don't get reviews is they never ask. Make it part of your workflow.

2. **Make it easy.** Create a direct review link using the Google Place ID. Send it via text message immediately after service.

3. **Timing matters.** Ask within 24 hours of completing the job. The longer you wait, the less likely they are to leave a review.

4. **Respond to every review.** Google sees responses as a signal of engagement. Thank positive reviewers, address negative ones professionally.

5. **Use QR codes.** Print a QR code that links directly to your review page. Put it on invoices, business cards, and leave-behind cards.

## What NOT to Do

- Don't buy fake reviews — Google detects and penalises this
- Don't offer incentives for reviews (against Google's guidelines)
- Don't ask family and friends to leave reviews
- Don't use review gating (only showing happy customers the review link)

## UK-Specific Tips

In the UK, Checkatrade and TrustATrader reviews also influence local rankings. Get listed on these platforms and encourage reviews there too.
