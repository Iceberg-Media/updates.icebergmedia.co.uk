Localo analysed 16,098 Google Business Profiles position-by-position. The results cut through years of SEO guesswork. Here's what actually matters — and what doesn't.

## The 8 Factors That Actually Rank

### 1. Review Count (Strongest Signal)

Median #1 profile: **79 reviews**. Median #11+: **25 reviews**.

The curve peaks at 201-500 reviews (73% top-3 rate), then flattens. Below 25 reviews, you're mostly off page one.

**Action:** Get more reviews than every competitor in your area. Total count is what matters — not pace, not length.

### 2. Photos (Only Factor With No Ceiling)

Median #1: **44 photos**. Median #11+: **21 photos**.

Photos are the only factor still climbing at 500+ images (73.2% top-3 rate). They separate #1 from #2 more sharply than any other metric.

**Action:** Upload 40+ photos. This is something you control — no waiting on customers.

### 3. Business Description (Compounds With Reviews)

Worth +10.8pp at 10 reviews, **+25.8pp at 200+ reviews**. Without a description, reviews stop paying past ~50.

**Action:** Write one. Length doesn't matter (100-200 chars performs same as 750). Just have one.

### 4. Additional Categories (6 Is the Number)

6 categories: **69.1% top-3 rate**. Zero categories: 47.9%.

**Action:** Add 6 secondary categories. This takes 5 minutes and moves the needle.

### 5. Citations (Plateau at 21-27)

Median #1: **27 citations**. Median #11+: **14 citations**.

Payoff front-loads hard. Past 30 citations, you're building for its own sake.

**Action:** Build to 21-27 citations on UK directories. Then stop and audit NAP consistency.

### 6. Social Media Links (5 Is Enough)

5 links: **68% top-3 rate**. Beyond 5, flat.

Facebook first (+13.2pp), then YouTube (+10.1pp).

**Action:** Add Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn. Done.

### 7. Google Posts (Publishing at All Matters)

Publishing: **70.8% top-3 rate**. Not publishing: 63.7%.

Content doesn't matter — keywords, length, frequency all flat. Only total post count matters (10+ active posts = 73%).

**Action:** Publish regularly. Don't obsess over optimisation.

### 8. Ratings (The 5.0 Paradox)

4.9 rating: **69.1% top-3 rate**. Perfect 5.0: **53.6%**.

Why? Perfect 5.0 usually means too few reviews. A 5.0 with 200+ reviews hits 76.8%.

**Action:** Don't panic over one bad review. 4.5+ is fine.

## The 6 Factors That Don't Matter

| Factor | Why It's Busted |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Review velocity** | Pace doesn't track with position — only total count does |
| **Review length** | 16-word reviews rank same as 100-word |
| **Reply rate** | Top profiles reply LESS (73.4% vs 76.7%) |
| **Reply length** | ~42 words at every position |
| **Description length** | 100-200 chars performs same as 750 |
| **Keywords in reviews** | Zero ranking impact |

## The Two Cliffs

| Transition | What Drops | Impact |
|------------|------------|--------|
| **#3 → #4** | Reviews -14, Photos -2 | Leave local pack |
| **#10 → #11+** | Reviews -17, Photos -6, Address -7pp | Leave page one |

## UK-Specific Context

These findings align with UK local search. The directories that matter most:

- **Yell** — strongest UK citation
- **Checkatrade** — reviews + citation combined
- **TrustATrader** — trade-specific authority
- **Thomson Local** — traditional but still indexed
- **Build UK** — construction/trade focused

## The Bottom Line

Reviews and photos are 5-10× more impactful than keyword optimisation. Focus there first.
