Your primary Google Business Profile category is the single biggest lever most businesses ignore. Pick the wrong one and you're invisible to your best customers.

## Why Category Matters So Much

Google uses your category to determine which searches you appear for. A plumber categorised as "Handyman" will miss most plumbing searches. A dentist categorised as "Doctor" will miss dental searches.

## How to Pick the Right Category

1. **Search for your primary service in your city.** Look at the top 3 businesses in the Map Pack. What category do they use?

2. **Match your highest revenue service.** If 80% of your revenue comes from boiler installations, your primary category should be "Boiler Installation Service" — not "Plumber."

3. **Use the most specific category available.** "Emergency Plumber" is better than "Plumber" if that's what you do.

4. **Check competitor categories.** Use Google Maps to see what categories your top competitors have selected.

## UK-Specific Categories

Google has UK-specific categories that aren't available in other countries. Make sure you're using the right ones for your market.

## Adding Secondary Categories

You can add up to 9 secondary categories. Use them for related services, but your primary category should always match your main revenue driver.
