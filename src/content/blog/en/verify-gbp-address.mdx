Trying to rank a Google Business Profile without a verified address is a waste of time in almost every UK market. Google won't show unverified businesses in the Map Pack.

## Why Verification Matters

Google's local algorithm only shows verified businesses in the Map Pack. An unverified GBP is essentially invisible to local searchers.

## Verification Methods

1. **Postcard verification** — Google sends a postcard with a PIN to your business address. Takes 5-14 days.

2. **Phone verification** — Available for some businesses. Google calls your business phone with a PIN.

3. **Email verification** — Available for some businesses. Google sends a PIN to your business email.

4. **Video verification** — New method. Record a video of your business location and signage.

## Service Area Businesses

If you're a service-area business (plumber, electrician, etc.), you still need a verified address. You can hide it from public display, but Google needs to verify you exist at a real location.

## Common Issues

- **Virtual offices don't work.** Google requires a physical address where you can receive mail.
- **PO boxes don't work.** Google specifically prohibits PO boxes.
- **Home addresses work** — but you can hide them from public display.
