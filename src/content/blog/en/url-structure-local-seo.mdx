/manchester-boiler-installation beats /services every time. URLs should tell Google exactly what a page is about.

## Why URL Structure Matters

Google uses URLs to understand page content. A descriptive URL is a ranking signal.

## How to Structure URLs

### Service Pages
- Bad: `/services/page-1`
- Good: `/boiler-installation-manchester`

### Location Pages
- Bad: `/areas/manchester`
- Good: `/plumber-manchester`

### Blog Posts
- Bad: `/blog/post-123`
- Good: `/blog/how-to-choose-a-plumber`

## Rules to Follow

1. **Use hyphens, not underscores.** Google treats hyphens as word separators.
2. **Keep URLs short.** Shorter URLs rank better.
3. **Include keywords.** Put your primary keyword in the URL.
4. **Lowercase only.** URLs are case-sensitive.
5. **No special characters.** Stick to letters, numbers, and hyphens.

## UK-Specific Tips

- Include city names in URLs
- Use UK spelling in URLs
- Include service area in URL structure
