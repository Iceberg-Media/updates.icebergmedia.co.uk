Embedding your Google Business Profile in press releases is an underrated tactic that signals authority and drives direct engagement.

## Why It Works

When you distribute press releases, the GBP link gets picked up by news sites and aggregators. This creates backlinks and citations that Google sees as authority signals.

## How to Do It

1. **Include your GBP link in every press release.** Add it in the company information section at the bottom.

2. **Use the full GBP URL.** Copy it directly from your Google Business Profile.

3. **Distribute through UK PR networks.** Use UK-specific PR distribution sites for maximum local impact.

4. **Include NAP+W.** Make sure your Name, Address, Phone, and Website are consistent with your GBP.

## UK PR Distribution Sites

- IssueWire
- PRWeb
- Newswire
- Pressat (UK-specific)

## What to Announce

- New services or products
- Awards or recognition
- Community involvement
- Seasonal promotions
- Staff announcements
