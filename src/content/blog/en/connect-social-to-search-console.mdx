Google now lets you connect YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and X as "platform properties" in Search Console. You can see which searches drive people to your social posts.

## What's New

Search Console has only ever tracked website traffic. Now Google added a new property type called "platform properties." You connect a social account and get the same data:
- Clicks
- Impressions
- Exact search terms driving people to your videos and posts

## Why This Matters for Local SEO

Google has been indexing and ranking social content for years. Search "AC replacement Phoenix" or "water heater installation near me" — there's a good chance a YouTube video is sitting on page one.

## How to Set It Up

1. Go to Google Search Console
2. Add a new property
3. Select "Platform properties"
4. Connect your YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, or X account
5. Verify ownership

## What to Do With the Data

- Find which searches drive traffic to your social posts
- Create more content around those topics
- Optimise your social profiles for local search
- Cross-reference with your website data
