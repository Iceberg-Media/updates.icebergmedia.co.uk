Your Name, Address, and Phone number must be identical everywhere — your website, GBP, Yell, Thomson Local, Checkatrade, TrustATrader, and every UK directory. Same formatting, same number, same postcode. Not optional.

## Why NAP Consistency Matters

Google cross-references your business information across the web. If your address is "14 Commercial St" on one site and "14 Commercial Street" on another, Google sees them as different businesses.

## What to Check

1. **Business name** — Must be identical everywhere
2. **Address** — Same format, same abbreviations
3. **Phone number** — Same number, same format
4. **Website** — Same URL (with or without www)

## UK-Specific NAP Rules

- Use consistent postcode format (M15 4PZ, not M154PZ)
- Use consistent street abbreviations (St vs Street)
- Use consistent phone format (+44 161 354 9017 vs 0161 354 9017)
- Include country code for international directories

## Where to Check

- Your website (header, footer, contact page)
- Google Business Profile
- Yell.com
- Thomson Local
- Checkatrade
- TrustATrader
- Yelp
- Facebook
- LinkedIn
