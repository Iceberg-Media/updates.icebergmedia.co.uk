Most UK trade websites have 200-word service pages and wonder why they don't rank. Your service page is a ranking asset with a job to do.

## The Perfect Service Page Structure

### H1 Tag
Lead with the service and city: "Boiler Installation in Manchester" — not "Welcome" or "Our Services."

### Separate Pages Per Service
Don't lump everything onto one general services page. Roof repair gets its own page. Roof replacement gets its own page.

### Length
Aim for 1,000+ words. Look at whoever ranks above you and beat their length. You're not padding — you're covering what they cover plus what they missed.

### Real Content
- Before and after photos of real jobs (not stock images)
- FAQ section covering cost, timeline, warranty
- Case studies from local customers
- Pricing information (even ranges)

### Local Signals
- Mention your service area
- Include local postcodes
- Reference local landmarks
- Add a Google Map embed

## UK-Specific Tips

- Use British English spelling
- Reference UK regulations and standards
- Include UK-specific pricing (in GBP)
- Mention local councils if relevant
